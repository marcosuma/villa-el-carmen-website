The villa

A peaceful base for exploring Puglia

Villa El Carmen is a private villa set in more than one hectare of olive groves in the countryside of San Vito dei Normanni, between Ostuni and the Adriatic coast. The house brings together a restored trullo and a traditional lamia with modern comforts: three bedrooms, three bathrooms, air conditioning and heating throughout, and fast Starlink WiFi.

It is made for families and friends who want one quiet base for their week in Puglia: mornings by the pool, afternoons on the beaches of Specchiolla and Torre Guaceto, evenings in the white town of Ostuni or around the outdoor table under the olive trees.

The stone heart

A restored trullo and a historic lamia

The stone heart of the villa: vaulted ceilings, thick walls that keep the rooms cool in summer, and the craftsmanship of the Apulian countryside.

The trullo

Our restored trullo keeps its original dry-stone dome and niches, now a quiet corner to read and rest. Trulli are the most recognisable buildings of Puglia, and having one all to yourself is part of the experience.

The lamia

The lamia, with its vaulted stone ceilings, is home to the kitchen and the dining room. Every room has been renovated with care, pairing traditional materials with modern comfort.

The pool

A 40 m² swimming pool with sun loungers, surrounded by lawn and olive trees and softly lit at night. The perfect place to cool off after a day of sightseeing.

One hectare of olive trees

The villa is surrounded by olive trees. Walk through the grove, enjoy privacy and silence, and taste the oil we make from these very trees.

Experiences

Apulian evenings and alfresco dining

An outdoor kitchen with barbecue, a table for ten and long summer evenings.

Liquid gold: our own olive oil

We make extra virgin olive oil from olives picked in our own grove. It is a gift from our land to your table. Every stay includes a complimentary bottle, and you can buy more to take home.

Aperitivo at sunset

Gather on the terrace as the sun goes down, with a spritz, local taralli and a board of cheese and cured meats.

Dinner under the olive trees

The covered outdoor dining area seats ten, next to a fully equipped outdoor kitchen and barbecue: made for long dinners with family and friends.

Private chef and cooking class

On request we can arrange a private chef or a hands-on cooking class at the villa: learn to make fresh orecchiette and taste traditional Apulian dishes.

Work from Puglia

Room to work, if you need it

Fast, reliable Starlink WiFi covers the whole property, and two dedicated desks, one of them a height-adjustable standing desk, look out over the olive grove.

Ideal for longer stays in spring and early autumn, when Puglia is quieter and the weather is still warm.

Location

Where we are

In the countryside of San Vito dei Normanni (Brindisi), between Ostuni and the Adriatic sea: quiet and private, yet within easy reach of Puglia's most beautiful towns and beaches.

A car is essential to explore the area: the villa is in the countryside and public transport is limited. We are happy to help you arrange a rental car or an airport transfer.

Beaches nearby

  • Specchiolla and Apani: sandy beaches and small rocky coves, about 20–24 minutes away.
  • Torre Guaceto: a protected nature reserve with dunes and crystal-clear water, about 24 minutes away.
  • The coast of Ostuni and, for a day trip, the Ionian beaches around Porto Cesareo, about 48 minutes away.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How far is the villa from the beach?

The nearest beaches, Specchiolla and Apani, are about 20–24 minutes by car, and the Torre Guaceto nature reserve about 24 minutes. The Ionian coast near Porto Cesareo is about 48 minutes away.

Do I need a car?

Yes. The villa is in the countryside and buses are infrequent, so a car is the best way to reach beaches, towns and restaurants. We can help you arrange a rental car or a transfer.

Which airport is closest?

Brindisi airport is about 24 minutes away. Bari airport, with more international flights, is about 1 hour 12 minutes away.

Is the pool private?

Yes. The 40 m² pool is for our guests only, with sun loungers and lighting for the evening.

Is the villa suitable for families with children?

Yes. Many of our guests are families with children: the house is spacious and the grounds are quiet and private.

Can I work remotely from the villa?

Yes. Starlink WiFi covers the whole property and there are two dedicated desks, one of them a standing desk.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is between 4 pm and 9 pm, and check-out is by 10 am.

When is the villa open, and what is the minimum stay?

The villa is open from April to early October. The minimum stay is 3 to 6 nights, depending on the season.

Are pets allowed?

We are sorry, but pets are not allowed.

How much does the villa cost per night?

In 2027 the whole villa costs from €420 to €740 per night depending on the season, with a minimum stay of 3 to 6 nights. Airbnb's fees are added when you book, and Airbnb shows the final price for your dates.

How do I book?

Bookings are made through Airbnb, where you can see live availability and prices. For any question before booking, write to us by email or on Instagram.
